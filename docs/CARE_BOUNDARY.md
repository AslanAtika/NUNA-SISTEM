# Self-Determination and Information Boundaries

This is a general design policy derived from the requested communication principles. The personal health essay is intentionally not bundled.

## Preserve voice without upgrading certainty

Keep observations, self-reports, interpretations, verified records, and open questions distinct. A saved user report is not a confirmed diagnosis; a fictional event is not proof of a real event.

User-entered `verified` labels are player classifications, not system or clinical certification. The prototype must not be represented as a clinical record or decision system.

## Consent and care

Being discussed with a support team does not by itself establish medical approval, a reviewed medication plan, or substance safety. Do not infer those conclusions or recommend medication changes from saved notes.

Support user choice, readable reasoning, and reversible interactions. Do not reward distress, require substance use, or treat creative expression as diagnostic evidence.

## Prototype limitations

This is a fictional puzzle prototype, not a medical device, care-team integration, diagnosis tool, or crisis monitor. No medical records, team permissions, or medication plans were checked.

Save files remain local and excluded from Git. Local storage is not encrypted; do not enter sensitive health details into the game. Never publish user notes without explicit, specific authorization.
