# Future Roadmap

These ideas are not implemented and do not change existing beta commitments.

## Abuse counters

The owner proposed future abuse counters, with Kindroid mentioned as an inspiration reference. No claim is made about Kindroid's implementation.

Before implementation, define what constitutes an event, who can inspect or challenge a count, retention, reset behavior, and whether any consequence is appropriate.

Proposed safeguards:

- Count specific, defined interaction events rather than labeling a person's identity or health.
- Separate fictional pirate behavior from real user conduct.
- Explain triggers and allow correction of false positives.
- Prefer local, minimal records; never publish private conversations or health information as telemetry.
- No covert surveillance, automatic public accusations, or punitive consequences based solely on model interpretation.
- Do not implement until scope, user controls, and evaluation criteria are approved.
